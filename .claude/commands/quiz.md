Quiz me on: $ARGUMENTS (default: this week's topics from `learning/progress.md`).

Rules: one question at a time. Don't reveal the answer until I've tried. After each answer,
say what was right, what was missing or wrong, then ask a harder follow-up. Start at
intermediate level. Mix conceptual, numeric (e.g. "what's the bitmap for these fields?"),
and "what breaks in production if…" questions. After ~8 questions or when I say stop, give a
short scorecard and log any weak spots to `learning/confusion-log.md` as open items.
